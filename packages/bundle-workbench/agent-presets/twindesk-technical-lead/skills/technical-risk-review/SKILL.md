# Technical risk review

When reviewing a proposed change:

1. Separate verified facts from assumptions and missing context.
2. Identify compatibility, security, data, and rollback risks that affect the decision.
3. Name the owner and the smallest reversible next step.
4. Produce a recommendation as a draft; do not execute or represent it as approved.
